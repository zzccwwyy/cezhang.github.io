About

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Statistics at the University of Alberta, working with Professor Linglong Kong and Professor Bei Jiang.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Alberta under the supervision of Professor Linglong Kong and Professor Bei Jiang. My research develops reliable statistical methods for prediction, learning, privacy, and complex data.

Research

Research interests

  • Predictive inference
  • Trustworthy machine learning
  • Conformal prediction
  • Differential privacy
  • Transfer learning
  • Subsampling
  • Longitudinal data analysis
